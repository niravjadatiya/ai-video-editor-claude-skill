# Example style note (generic) — talking-head explainer reel, 9:16
Format: ≤25 lines. Generated by the kit from a reference reel; reuse with `style: <name>`.
- **Pacing:** single talking-head take, few hard cuts; rhythm from graphics swapping every ~5–8s.
- **Hook:** first 0–5s = bold 2-line title card in the top third, clear of the face.
- **Layout:** split-screen for proof moments (panel on top, speaker bottom); full-frame when just talking.
- **Captions:** lower-middle (~75% height), 2–4 words, white on dark pill, no karaoke highlight.
- **Typography:** bold sans; accent colour for 1–2 keywords per card.
- **Graphics:** one idea per panel; highlights appear on the spoken number.
- **Motion:** minimal; panels slide/pop in, no flashy transitions.
- **Audio:** voice-first, soft music bed optional, SFX every 3–5s.
- **Safe zones:** captions above platform UI.
- **Don't borrow:** the reference's logos, data, footage or exact wording.
